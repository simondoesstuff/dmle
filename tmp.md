⏺ From step_gaussian in train_hnn.py + \_task_loss (default lambda_target_decay=0) + the new entropy_loss:

$$
\mathcal{L}\text{gauss} = \underbrace{\frac{1}{B}\sum{b=1}^{B}\bigl(\hat{y}b - y_b\bigr)^2}{\text{task MSE}} + ;\lambda_\text{entropy}\underbrace{\sum_{i=1}^{32}
e^{-\sigma_i^*}}_{\text{narrowness penalty}}
$$

where $\sigma_i^* = \log\sigma_i$ (log_bandwidths[i]), so $e^{-\sigma_i^*} = 1/\sigma_i$.

The prediction $\hat{y}_b$ flows through the full frozen pipeline:

$$\hat{y}b = h_T^{(b)}[0], \qquad h_T = \text{RNNcell}^{\times 10}(h_0 = c\text{enc}^{(b)})$$

where the RNN cell weights are generated by the coord net from the current stimulus:

$$\text{stimulus} = \exp!\left(-\frac{|z - \mu_i|^2}{2,\sigma_i^2,T}\right)_{i=1}^{32}$$

Only centers and log*bandwidths receive gradients; z, stim_ffn, and param_embeddings are frozen constants in this phase. Default: $\lambda*\text{entropy} = 0.001$.
